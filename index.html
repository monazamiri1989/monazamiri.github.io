About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Wayne State University and a researcher in the TEANA Group. My research focuses on conversational information seeking over knowledge graphs, with an emphasis on neural retrieval and ranking models.

My work combines natural language processing, graph neural networks, and structured knowledge to improve conversational entity retrieval. I am currently investigating reinforcement learning and LLM-based query transformation to improve downstream retrieval performance.

My research has appeared at WSDM and The ACM Web Conference (WWW). I am interested in practical AI/ML engineering applications and welcome opportunities for research collaboration.
